DTM Nigeria Data Hub

All data IOM DTM Nigeria publishes on dtm.iom.int and ReliefWeb — mobility tracking rounds, emergency tracking, incident and flash reports, and every publication — cross-checked against the curated data files. Choose a region, state or period on the left and every number, table and chart updates.

How to use the dashboard

  1. Pick a place and period. In the blue panel, choose a region, a state and the years. Mobility tracking has its own round selector.
  2. Move between rounds. On Mobility tracking, click a round in the coverage strip (shift-click sets the start), or pick Round A and Round B to see the change for every state and LGA in percentages.
  3. Follow trends. Choose states or LGAs as chips to follow them across rounds, weeks or months.
  4. Check the sources. Every round links to its report and dataset online; the cross-check table compares the data files with the published figures.
  5. Read ETT and flash reports by week and month. Switch Week / Month / Year and State / LGA on those pages.
  6. Export. Every table has Download CSV; “Save offline copy” keeps the whole platform in one file that works without internet.
  7. Ask the data. Use the button at the bottom right, for example “returnees in Adamawa round 44” or “flash reports per month in Zamfara 2025”.
  8. Add new rounds. North East R53–R65 and North Central & North West R20–R35 can be added as files or typed-in figures on Add data.

What each component covers

Good to know

Rounds are shown from the lowest to the highest. Round-to-round change in households and individuals is always shown as a percentage. Values not yet published or harvested show as a dash — nothing is estimated. Sites (IDP locations, camps) are counts stated in reports; the data files are ward-level, so dataset rounds also show wards with IDPs.

Mobility tracking data files – several rounds at once

Choose or drop as many Excel/CSV files as you like (for example North East R47–R65 and North Central & North West R20–R35). Each file can hold one or many rounds and several sheets (IDPs, camps, host communities, returnees). The platform reads the round, state, LGA, ward, location, households and individuals columns and works out, for every round and population group, total households, total individuals, and distinct LGAs, wards and locations assessed.

To make them permanent for everyone, upload the same files to GitHub: open the data/raw upload page. Name them ne_mobility_….xlsx or nwnc_mobility_….xlsx.

Sources & method

Where the numbers come from

The datasets are the ward-level Mobility Tracking files (North East and North Central & North West), the Emergency Tracking Tool records and the post-flood community assessments. The web harvester crawls dtm.iom.int/nigeria, the dtm.iom.int sitemap, response.reliefweb.int/nigeria/data, the ReliefWeb API and HDX, sorts every publication into a component, and reads headline figures, LGAs and communities out of the report text and PDFs. Files and figures added on the “Add data” tab are merged on top.

Rounds without a dataset are filled from the figures stated in the published reports. Values not yet available show as a dash; nothing is estimated.

Locations

IDP locations, camps and host-community locations are site counts stated in reports. The datasets are ward-level, so for dataset rounds the platform also counts wards with IDPs and wards with camp IDPs; return locations are counted from the named return locations in the datasets. North East rounds 1–11 also covered several north-central and north-west states; those figures appear under their states.

ETT and incidents

ETT weeks are ISO weeks (Monday–Sunday), matching the weekly ETT reports. Locations receiving arrivals are distinct ward/location pairs in each period. For flash reports that cover several states or LGAs, displaced figures are split equally between them in state and LGA views.
